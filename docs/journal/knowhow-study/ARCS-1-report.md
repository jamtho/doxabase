Commissioned by James's digest critique ("watch the growth and

## The headline: the critique is vindicated with specifics

Decisions-as-punctuation held for arc INTERIORS (11/13 arcs route
through the prose cross-references) but **fails at both ends**:

- **Arc onsets are systematically unrecorded.** The Jan-1 anomaly —
  "2,736 of 2,738 name transitions land exactly on Jan 1," the seed of
  the study's most famous arc and the centerpiece of the public case
  study — has NO episode among all 249, because an anomaly is not a
  decision. The decision-lens harvest structurally could not see
  beginnings.
- **Arc termini are the absence of punctuation**: completion shows as
  decisions STOPPING (doctrine stated pre-build; a second domain
  mapped "without re-derivation"; cold-agent restraint) — filed as
  mere "texture" by the decision lens.
- Comprehension checkpoints and question-quality evolution (round 1:
  "is it my index builder?" → round 9: design-layer questions) are
  invisible to all eleven episode shapes.

## Modeling recommendation (for the tranche-2 distiller, with James)

Arcs as **rc:Pattern-based syntheses** (kh:Arc ⊑ rc:Pattern), NOT a
parallel class system — argued from instances: the pattern class's
own semantics ("a synthesis over observations... bridges point-in-time
findings and the map") fit exactly; all 13 arcs were reconstructed
with zero new capture machinery. Prerequisite: type the 41 prose
cross-references. Property candidates clearing the bar many times
over: ordered membership; pivot-quote (13/13); became-automatic
(13/13). The ladder was the understanding-model all along — the
know-how layer needs less invention than either party assumed.

Also settled empirically: the narrated-monologue modelling question
and the understanding-lens question are one question — every pivot
quote was found in visible narration; decisions are its punctuation.
