   ≥2 corpora, mechanically visible. A per-genre namespace scheme was REFUSED: it would fork
   the vocabulary the way t5-17 forbids forking machinery, and the gradient is a property of
   evidence, not of names.

   diagnosisExchange, processNormEnacted, machineReviewOutcome, maintainerOverride; email —
   scopedDelegationRequest, counterpartyPositionHold, precedentConformanceCheck,

   61 kernel episodes (fidelity over invention).
