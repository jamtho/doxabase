# The Know-How Study — public working documents

The know-how generalization pilot (design doc 15) applied DoxaBase's
own machinery to a new target: the tacit design know-how of this
project itself. These are the working documents mature enough to
share — the reader interested in HOW we work will find the most here:

- `ARCS-1-report.md` — the understanding-arc re-analysis: why the
  decision-lens harvest missed the beginnings and ends of
  understanding, the thirteen reconstructed arcs, and the finding
  that an arc completes when its decisions stop being decisions.
- `VOCAB-NOTE-KH2.md` — the vocabulary's understanding-lens revision,
  worked examples first: the identity-freeze arc end-to-end from raw
  transcript chunk to queryable pattern node.
- `DISTILL-2.md` — the distiller's own working journal for that
  revision.

Phase 1 of the corpus program (2026-08-11/12) added the
foreign-corpus documents: the two scout memos and the density sample
(`enron-scout-memo.md`, `syzbot-scout-memo.md`,
`syzbot-sample-memo.md` — reconnaissance with GO/NO-GO verdicts and
the conditions that governed the harvests), the two pilot journals
(`ENRON-PILOT-1.md`, `SYZBOT-PILOT-1.md` — the first harvests of
email and kernel-review corpora into their own kh-seeded capsules),
and `CROSS-CORPUS-SYNTHESIS.md` — the capstone: the generalization
verdict graded across three corpora, the docket revival check, and
the ranked distiller-3 agenda. Named individuals appearing in these
documents are quoted from public-record corpora (FERC-released email;
public kernel mailing lists) with attribution, per research norms; no
person-model claims about living public figures are made or licensed
by them. `PROMOTE-1.md` (earlier) records the first heuristic
promotions and the consented owner person-model build.

Trial mechanics (the sealed A/B and its blind judging) are one level
up in `docs/journal/trials/`. The underlying episode corpus profiles
the project's human participant closely and deliberately lives
outside this repository; these documents reference it by IRI. The
addressed-to-the-owner voice in the notes is preserved rather than
edited away — the correspondence is the method.
