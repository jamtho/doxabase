## The load-bearing detail

The judge's "best idea in Design 1" — detecting that an invariant's
constrainedBy target belongs to ANOTHER contract and rendering it as
"reused by reference" with an owner lookup — is directly
capsule-sourced: B's text cited the harvested episode recording the
doc-12 decision (share evidence by citation, never duplicate parameter
resources). The baseline design BROKE on exactly that case (its
parameter pills dead-end on M13's cross-contract reuse — a verified
real breakage on real capsule data). The information existed in the
raw repo corpus available to BOTH agents; only the capsule-equipped
agent surfaced and applied it. That is the null hypothesis
("the raw corpus is enough") failing in the most specific possible
way: distillation made the decisive episode findable.
